Error strata
ART is only valid against its own residual, so each stratum is fitted separately. Stratum A (20 plot means, 9 df) tests Harvest, Species and Genotype(Species); stratum B (~405 plants, ~350 df) tests Time and its interactions.
Power for genotype
With 2 harvests and one plot per genotype, Error A has only 9 df. An "ns" in that stratum means the design cannot demonstrate the difference, not that there is none.
Fitted model
Chosen once per variable among linear, quadratic, exponential, logarithmic and Weibull, fitted to all individual plants. The linear model is kept unless another form beats it by more than 2.0 AICc units. Linear reads as zero-order degradation, exponential as first order.
k
[C(DAH 14) − C(DAH 2)] / 12 — the mean rate of change over the whole storage window, re-estimated per species with a mixed model (genotype as random effect).
Δ_R, AUC_d, CV
Net relative change (% of DAH 2); mean sustained absolute deviation, which penalises non-monotonic trajectories; and plant-to-plant coefficient of variation.
Threshold
mean(DAH 2) + 0.5 × [mean(DAH 14) − mean(DAH 2)], per species — 50% of the observed change, averaged first within genotype and then across the genotypes of the species. These are the dotted lines in the kinetic figures.
SI (0–100)
Stability index combining four percentile-ranked metrics: normalised slope, Δ_R, AUC_d and CV. Higher = more stable.
Moisture
Measured in harvest 2 only (n = 199): Harvest, Species and Genotype are confounded with plot and not estimable.
Pyruvic acid
Experiment 1 only, two sampling times (n = 97), so no kinetic model, threshold or stability index.
Multiple testing
17 variables × 6 effects = 102 tests with no multiplicity correction. Isolated p-values just below 0.05 should be read with caution; effects at p<0.001 across many variables are unaffected.
